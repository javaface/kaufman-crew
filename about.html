About

This site is for the Kaufman Crew — a group of regulars who play together in Wyoming, MI. It tracks course announcements (closures, conditions, aeration windows) and upcoming tee times and pairings.

This is an unofficial, privately maintained site and is not affiliated with any golf course or club.

Scores are tracked separately via GHIN.

How to Update the Site

Edit _data/announcements.yml or _data/teetimes.yml on GitHub and commit. The site rebuilds automatically.

Data Files

All content is stored as YAML in the _data/ folder:

FileContents
_data/announcements.ymlCourse status updates
_data/teetimes.ymlUpcoming and past rounds with pairings

Announcement Fields

FieldRequiredDescription
titleYesShort headline
dateYesYYYY-MM-DD
typeYesClosure, Cart Path Only, Aerification, Weather, Event, General
bodyYesFull announcement text
expiresNoYYYY-MM-DD — card hidden after this date
sourceNoURL to official post

Tee Time Fields

FieldRequiredDescription
dateYesYYYY-MM-DD
timeYesHH:MM (24-hour)
holesYes9 or 18
notesNoAny extra context
pairingsNoList of groups, each with a players list
